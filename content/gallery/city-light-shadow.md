The city I live in is not large, but its light and shadow are different every day.

In the morning, sunlight squeezes through gaps between buildings and draws golden lines across the streets. At noon, light falls straight from above and shadows shrink beneath your feet. By evening, the sunset turns the whole city orange-red, and even glass facades begin to look gentle.

I took this set of photos on my way to and from work. There was no careful composition, only moments when the light looked good enough to make me stop and press the shutter.

Life is never short of beauty. What is often missing is the willingness to stop and look.
