>  Astrism along with Prayatna conducted an outreach event at Gur Aasra Orphanage for girls of all ages with a simple goal: to make science accessible, fun and aspiring

We demonstrated Sunspots using Astromaster 130 EQ equipped with a Solar Filter.
We put together interactive fun posters to explain the concept behind sunspots.

Here’s to breaking down barriers, sharing knowledge, and inspiring the next generation of female scientists!
