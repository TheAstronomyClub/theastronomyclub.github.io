This gallery uses **local images only**. It demonstrates the custom gallery workflow: photos sit next to `index.md`, with no image hosting and no repeated links pasted into the document.

Morning light enters through the window at an angle and moves across the floor pane by pane. I made tea, sat in the light, and watched dust drift through the beam.

I saved many quiet mornings like this with a camera. A gallery does not need many photos; three can be enough to tell a small story. The writing does not need to be long either. A note about the light, the temperature, and the mood is enough.

> Drop photos into the folder, use this Markdown file to tell the story behind them, and the gallery is done.
