## Online Resources

- [Astrobites!](https://astrobites.org/) - This is a daily astrophysical literature journal written by graduate students in astronomy. They present recent exciting astro research in brief format that is accessible to undergraduate students.
- [Intro2Astro](https://sites.google.com/view/intro-2-astro?authuser=1) - An introductory cost-free online course targeted at aspiring students, researchers, and enthusiasts to step foot in the world of astronomy research. This covers coding skills with soft skills such as web development, scientific paper reading, and CV creation.
- [Sagan Exoplanet Workshops](https://nexsci.caltech.edu/workshop/) - Conducted at Caltech each year. All the lectures and hands-on sessions are available on youtube and the website. Goldmine for peeps interested in exoplanets.
- [Code/Astro](https://semaphorep.github.io/codeastro/) - A week-long astronomy software development workshop motivated by the growing usage of open-source software in astronomical research
